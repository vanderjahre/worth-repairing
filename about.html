About the Author

Dave Genu is a mysterious figure in the world of webcomics, known only by his online pseudonym. He is rumored to be a former circus performer who now spends his days writing and illustrating comics from a secluded cabin in the woods. His work often explores themes of redemption, found family, and the quiet moments that make life worth living.
